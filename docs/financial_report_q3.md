# Helix Manufacturing — Q3 2025 Financial Report

Revenue for Q3 2025 reached $4.2M, up 18% year over year, driven primarily by
the industrial sensors division. Gross margin improved to 41% from 37% in Q2.

Operating expenses were $1.1M, with R&D accounting for $620K. The board
approved updated Q4 revenue guidance of $4.8M, contingent on the Phoenix
production line reaching full capacity by November.

Headcount at quarter end was 212 employees, including 64 in engineering.
