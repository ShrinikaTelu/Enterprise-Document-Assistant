# Phoenix Production Line — Technical Overview

The Phoenix line is Helix's automated assembly line for the HS-400 sensor
family, targeting a throughput of 1,400 units per shift at full capacity.

Commissioning began in July 2025. As of September, the line runs at 60%
capacity pending calibration of the optical inspection stage. Full capacity
is projected for November 2025.

The line integrates 14 robotic cells, an inline vision QA system with a
0.4% target defect-escape rate, and OPC-UA telemetry streamed to the plant
historian every 500 ms.
