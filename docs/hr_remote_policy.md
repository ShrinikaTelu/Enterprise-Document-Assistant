# Helix Manufacturing — Remote Work Policy (rev. 2025-08)

Engineering and corporate staff may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
Plant-floor and quality-assurance roles are on-site only.

Remote days must be coordinated within teams so that at least 50% of each
team is on-site on any given weekday. Core collaboration hours are
10:00–15:00 local plant time.

Equipment: the company provides one laptop and one monitor for home use.
A one-time $300 home-office stipend is available after the probation period.
